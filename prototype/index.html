            Tighter accuracy (optional)

            One sheet pins the floor down well near itself and poorly far from it. A second known size, lying out where you are measuring, tightens the far numbers.

            A paper size and a typed length are taken as exact. Nothing on this page checks them. If one is wrong, the numbers near it are wrong with it, and their error bars will not show it. The page refuses only when two references disagree by far more than tap error explains, which catches a gross mistake near the first sheet and little else. No refusal does not mean the references are right.
